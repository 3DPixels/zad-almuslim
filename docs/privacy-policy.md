**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the زاد المسلم app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Ammar Ahmad (collectively, the "Application"). Ammar Ahmad is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

**Information Collection and Use**
The Service Provider does not collect, store, or transmit personal information, behavioral analytics, or usage statistics (such as pages visited, time spent, or IP addresses). Any data required for the Application to function properly—such as your last known coordinates used to calculate offline prayer times—is cached entirely locally on your device and is never transmitted to the Service Provider.

**Location Information**
The Application requests access to your device's approximate location solely for the purpose of calculating accurate, localized Islamic prayer times and determining your city name. This process utilizes native device services (such as Google Play Services). The Service Provider does not collect, store, or transmit your location data to any personal external servers or third-party marketing companies.

**Local Notifications**
The Application requests permission to schedule exact alarms in order to deliver timely prayer and azkar notifications. These notifications are scheduled, triggered, and managed entirely locally by your device's operating system. No data regarding your notification preferences or schedule is transmitted to the Service Provider.

**Third Party Access**
The Application does not share, sell, or transmit your data to third-party analytics or advertising networks. The only third-party interaction occurs through your device's native operating system (specifically Google Play Services) solely for the purpose of reverse-geocoding (converting your coordinates into a readable city name). 

Below is the link to the Privacy Policy of the third-party service provider utilized natively by the Application:
*   [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Your Rights & California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)**
Under applicable privacy laws (including CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. 

**However, please note:** Because the Application operates entirely offline and the Service Provider does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data in the first place, we do not hold any data to provide, modify, or delete, nor do we sell or share data.

**Opt-Out Rights**
Because all data processing happens locally, you can stop all local data processing at any time by revoking the location and notification permissions in your device settings, or by simply uninstalling the Application. 

**Children**
The Application is not intended to, and does not, collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. 

**Security**
Because the Service Provider does not collect or store any of your personal data on external servers, your data is not at risk of being breached from our systems. The security of your locally cached data relies entirely on the security of your device and its operating system.

**Changes**
The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The Service Provider will notify you of material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy with an effective date.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-10.
